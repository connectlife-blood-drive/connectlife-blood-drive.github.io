STEP BY STEPHow to Donate

1

Make sure you can donate

Students must:

  • Be at least 17 years old
  • Weigh at least 120 pounds

Teachers & staff: you're welcome to donate too!

2

Sign up with 2nd Period Marketing

Go to Room 123 for more details and to get signed up, or stop by our sign-up table in the Media Center during lunches, November 3–6.

Tell them you're signing up with 2nd Period Marketing. Every sign-up counts toward our class competition!

3

Have an appointment

You need an appointment to donate. If you're not sure you have one, ask in Room 123.

4

Get your 2 periods approved

You need 2 teacher-approved periods. You'll get a color-coded donor pass. Have your teachers sign it.

5

Donate on Friday, November 6

The drive runs 7:30 AM – 1:15 PM (1st – 7th period) in the Media Center.

6

Get your rewards

Every donor gets a free Sabres winter hat and a chance to win Sabres tickets.

THE IMPACTWhy It Matters

  • 1 blood donor can save up to 3 lives right here in Western New York.
  • ConnectLife is WNY's only community blood bank.
  • Every donation made with ConnectLife stays in Western New York.
